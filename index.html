MiSTer Monitor — touch test build (CYD 3.5" resistive)

Test build of 2.11.1 with two new config.ini options, touch_invert_x and touch_invert_y, for 3.5" resistive boards whose touch lands mirrored from the buttons on screen. With both options off it behaves exactly like 2.11.1.

Enable the option

Add this under the [ui] section of config.ini, either on the SD card or from the display's web editor at http://<display-ip>:8080/config, then reboot: 

touch_invert_y=true

If taps also land mirrored left to right, add touch_invert_x=true as well.

What to check

With flip_display=false and then flip_display=true:

  • PRV, SCN and NXT in the footer
  • GAME INFO page: tapping above the footer toggles 1/2 and 2/2
  • RetroAchievements list: row taps open the right row
  • Tap to leave standby, and tap to bring the footer back with kiosk_mode=true

The serial monitor (115200 baud) prints [TOUCH] Inverted axes at boot when the option is read, and the coordinates of every tap.
